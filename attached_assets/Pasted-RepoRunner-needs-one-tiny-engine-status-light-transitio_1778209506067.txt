## Current issue

When the light changes from:

```text
STOPPING
```

to:

```text
STOPPED
```

the `STOPPED` text briefly flashes white.

I do **not** want the text to flash white.

## Requested fix

### 1. Remove the text flash

Prevent the `STOPPED` text from flashing white during the transition.

The text should remain controlled/subtle and should not pop bright white.

The text can change from `STOPPING` to `STOPPED`, but it should not visually flash.

### 2. Add a quick red light flicker before shutdown

Instead of the text flashing, I want the **red light itself** to do a very quick final flash as it turns off.

Think of an old physical indicator light losing power:

```text
red light fades down → quick tiny red flicker → light goes dark
```

The effect should feel like:

* old hardware light turning off
* brief power-cut flicker
* quick red blink from the lens
* subtle but noticeable
* physical, not digital

## Timing

The final flicker should be quick.

Suggested direction:

```text
0.08s–0.18s
```

It should happen right before or as the status settles into `STOPPED`.

## Important details

The flicker should affect:

* red lens brightness
* internal glow
* maybe a tiny outer glow

The flicker should **not** affect:

* text brightness
* layout
* status logic
* light size
