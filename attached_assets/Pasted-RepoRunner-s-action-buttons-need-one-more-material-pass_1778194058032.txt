What to improve

Please increase the sense of glossy glass by refining these details:

1. Stronger specular highlight

Add a more visible reflective highlight across the upper portion of each button.

This should feel like a clean light reflection on glass, not a flat white band.

2. Better light-to-dark surface transition

The button face should have clearer tonal separation:

brighter toward the top / highlight zone
slightly deeper / darker toward the lower half

This helps create a real 3D glossy finish.

3. Sharper glass edge definition

Improve the rim lighting / edge treatment so the buttons feel more like layered glass or acrylic.

A subtle inner edge and faint outer edge highlight is fine.

4. Slightly stronger depth

Increase the feeling that the button has a real face/surface, not just a flat fill.

This can come from:

subtle inset highlight
soft lower shadow
gentle internal shading
slightly more convincing surface curvature
5. Keep the shine tasteful

Make the buttons noticeably glossier, but still premium.

Do not make them:

cartoonish
wet-looking
overly white
overly bright
plastic toy-like
neon
garish

The look should be:

dark
premium
cinematic
controlled
high-end
