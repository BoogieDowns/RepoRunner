When a status light says:


* be steadily illuminated red
* have a visible internal red glow
* have a subtle steady outer red glow
* look like a lit plastic indicator module
* not pulse
* not animate repeatedly

When a status light says:

```text
STARTING
STOPPING
```

it should:

* be steadily illuminated peach
* have a visible internal peach glow
* have a subtle steady outer peach glow
* look like a lit plastic indicator module
* not pulse
* not animate repeatedly

### STOPPED

When a status light says:

```text
STOPPED
```

it should remain:

* dark
* smoked
* unlit
* static

I want:

```text
active state = steady illuminated light
```

