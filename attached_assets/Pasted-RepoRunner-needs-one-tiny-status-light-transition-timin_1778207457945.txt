```text

```

* `RUNNING` red light
* `STARTING` peach light

```text
off/unlit → illuminates over 0.7s → stays steadily lit
```

When a light turns off and returns to `STOPPED`, it should turn off very quickly in about:

```text
0.1s
```

Desired behavior:

```text
active/lit → turns off over 0.1s → stopped/unlit
```

Use different transition timing for:

```text
turning on: 0.7s
turning off: 0.1s
```

* quick but still visible when powering on
