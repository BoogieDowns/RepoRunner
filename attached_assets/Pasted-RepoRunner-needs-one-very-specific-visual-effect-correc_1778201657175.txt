## Problem

Right now, when a meteor hits the horizontal divider line above the terminal/logs section, the effect is not reading correctly.

It currently feels like:
- a weak flash
- unclear ripple
- confusion between ripple and pixelation

The ripple is either too subtle or visually wrong.

## What I want instead

Use the reference direction conceptually:

- a meteor comes down and hits the horizontal divider line
- there is a bright red impact flash at the exact contact point
- the divider line itself visibly ripples / oscillates outward from the impact point
- after the meteor passes through the divider line, the meteor becomes heavily pixelated in the lower terminal zone

## Important distinction

The ripple is **not**:
- a particle burst
- a flare pretending to be a ripple
- just a glowing bar
- just a red blur

The ripple **is**:
- a visible wave deformation in the horizontal line itself
- like a signal wave or oscillation travelling outward from the impact point
- strongest near the center
- gradually diminishing as it expands left and right

Think:
- impact point flash
- then a wave in the line
- then the meteor continues through and becomes pixelated below the line

## Desired effect structure

### 1. Impact flash
When the meteor touches the divider line:

- create a clearly visible red impact flash at the exact contact point
- this should be brighter and more visible than it is now
- it should feel like a small concentrated burst of energy
- quick, sharp, and readable
- brief but obvious

### 2. Divider line ripple
Immediately after impact:

- the horizontal divider line should visibly distort into a ripple/wave pattern centered on the hit point
- the wave should expand left and right along the line
- the wave should be clearly visible, not subtle to the point of disappearing
- the center should have the strongest oscillation
- the oscillation amplitude should taper off as it moves outward
- the ends should fade smoothly and dissipate

Visually, this should read like:
- a line being struck
- a signal ripple travelling through it
- a waveform-like reaction

Do not make this just a flat glowing bar.
It must feel like the line itself is rippling.

### 3. Pixelation after crossing the line
Only after the meteor passes through the divider line into the terminal area:

- the meteor should become obviously pixelated
- use larger, more visible pixel blocks
- not tiny subtle fragments
- it should be clearly readable as a digital/pixelated transition
- keep it tasteful, but obvious enough to notice

The pixelation should happen below the divider line, not instead of the ripple.

## Separation of effects

Please keep these as three distinct beats:

1. meteor hits line
2. impact flash + visible line ripple
3. meteor continues below and becomes pixelated

Do not blend them so much that the ripple disappears.
