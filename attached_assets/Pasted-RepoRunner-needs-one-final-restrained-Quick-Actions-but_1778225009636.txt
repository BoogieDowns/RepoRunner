* icon/text gap improved

## Goal

Make the Quick Actions buttons feel more like premium physical glass controls.

They should look:

* slightly deeper
* more reflective
* more dimensional
* more expensive
* more finished
* still restrained and dark

Do not make them flashy or cartoonish.

## 1. Improve overall button depth

Add a little more three-dimensional depth to both red and neutral buttons.

Good areas to refine:

* lower inner shadow
* subtle bevel edge
* internal face gradient
* bottom darkening
* side rim highlight
* faint surface reflection

The buttons should feel like they have a glass face and a body beneath it.

---

The top highlight is better than before, but it can still be more elegant.

Please make the reflection feel more like a curved glass surface:

* not a harsh flat strip
* not too white
* softly blended
* slightly more shaped
* more realistic

Keep it visible, but premium.
