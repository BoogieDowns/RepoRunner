## What I want

The current status treatment should stay conceptually the same:

* the **status text remains inside the indicator**
* it still reads things like:

  * `Running`
  * `Starting`
  * `Stopping`
  * `Stopped`

But instead of a **pill**, I want it to look like an **old square/rectangular plastic indicator light**.

## Desired visual direction

Think of it like:

* an old industrial plastic status light
* compact and slightly rectangular
* not pill-shaped
* slightly squared corners
* realistic translucent red plastic lens
* detailed horizontal or ribbed lens lines / grooves / striations
* subtle depth, as if it is a real physical plastic light
* slightly aged industrial feel, but still clean and premium

It should feel like a small **lit plastic module** with the word overlaid on top.

## Important clarification

The **text should stay inside the light**.

So for example:

* `Running` should appear **inside** the red square/rectangular light
* `Starting` should appear **inside** the light
* `Stopping` should appear **inside** the light
* `Stopped` should appear **inside** the darker unlit version

Do not move the text outside.
Do not separate the text from the indicator.

## Shape

Please change the shape from pill to something like:

* square-ish or rectangular
* compact
* slightly rounded corners at most
* more like a small plastic status module
* not fully sharp, but definitely **not pill-shaped**

## Surface detail

The lens should have more realism:

* fine horizontal lens lines or ridges
* subtle internal highlights
* a plastic-looking face
* a little depth and realistic shading
* restrained gloss
* subtle glow when active

I want it to feel more like a **physical red warning/status light** rather than a flat UI pill.
