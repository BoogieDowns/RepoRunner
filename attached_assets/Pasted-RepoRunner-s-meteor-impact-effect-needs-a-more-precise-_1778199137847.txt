RepoRunner’s meteor impact effect needs a more precise correction.

The current effect seems to be getting confused with the pixelation effect.

Right now, when a meteor hits the horizontal divider line:

* it looks mostly like a pixelated flare at the impact point
* the line itself does not visibly ripple/react enough
* then the meteor continues downward and pixelates

I want these to be two clearly separate visual moments:

1. **Impact on the divider line:** the horizontal line ripples/flashes red.
2. **After passing through the line:** the meteor becomes heavily pixelated.

Do **not** redesign the UI.
Do **not** change layout.
Do **not** change functionality.
Do **not** change meteor spawn rate, fall speed, lifecycle, buttons, logs, cards, setup modal, IPC, or product flow.

This is only about separating and clarifying the **divider ripple** versus **meteor pixelation**.

---

## 1. Divider impact should be a line ripple, not a particle flare

When the meteor head reaches the horizontal divider line above the terminal/log section, the divider line itself should visibly react.

The effect should be on the **line**, not just around the meteor.

### Desired divider effect

At the impact point, create a subtle but noticeable red ripple travelling along the horizontal divider line.

It should look like:

* the line briefly lights up red at the impact point
* a wave/pulse travels left and right along the line
* the line itself appears energized by the hit
* the pulse fades softly at the ends

Think:

* a small electrical pulse travelling through a wire
* a red waveform moving along the divider
* a line-based ripple, not an explosion

### Important

Do **not** make this effect look like:

* a particle burst
* pixel fragments
* a red blob
* a flare detached from the line

The divider ripple should be visually anchored to the horizontal line.

---

## 2. Make the actual line ripple stronger

The current line ripple is still too weak.

Please make the line reaction more prominent.

Good approaches:

* briefly overlay a red glowing segment directly on top of the divider line
* animate the segment expanding left/right from the impact point
* add a slight vertical shimmer or thicker pulse on the line itself
* use a soft red glow that follows the line
* keep the center brightest and fade toward the ends

The line should visibly “ring” when hit.

### Suggested visual structure

At impact:

```text
grey divider line
        ↓ meteor hits
grey line briefly becomes red/glowy around impact
red pulse expands left and right along the line
pulse fades out
```

The ripple should feel clearly different from the pixel fragments.

---

## 3. Pixelation should happen after the meteor passes through the line

Once the meteor crosses below the divider and enters the terminal/log section, then the meteor should become heavily pixelated.

This part is separate from the line ripple.

### Desired pixelation

Below the divider:

* the meteor body/tail breaks into obvious square pixels
* larger red pixel chunks appear in the terminal section
* the effect follows the meteor downward
* it should not replace the divider ripple

The pixelation should not happen so strongly **on the divider line itself** that it hides the line ripple.

If needed:

* delay the pixel fragments by a tiny amount after impact
* spawn the pixel fragments slightly below the divider line
* keep the divider ripple visually clean and line-based

---

## 4. Keep both effects, but separate them clearly

The sequence should read like this:

```text
Meteor falls
Meteor hits divider
Divider line flashes/ripples red
Meteor passes below divider
Meteor becomes chunky/pixelated in terminal area
```

The user should be able to tell:

* “the line reacted”
* then “the meteor digitized”

Right now those two effects feel blended together.

---

## 5. Preserve current good behavior

Keep:

* meteor spawn from above viewport
* layered meteor depth
* current spawn rate
* current slower fall speed
* stop behavior
* existing meteor cleanup
* current pixelated look below the divider, but reposition/tune it if needed
* dark black/red aesthetic

Do not change unrelated UI.

---

## Acceptance criteria

After this fix:

1. When a meteor hits the divider, the horizontal line itself clearly ripples/flashes red.
2. The ripple is line-based and expands left/right along the divider.
3. The ripple does not look like pixel fragments or a particle flare.
4. Pixelation happens after the meteor crosses below the divider.
5. Pixel fragments appear clearly in the terminal/log area, not primarily on top of the divider line.
6. The sequence reads as: impact ripple first, pixelation second.
7. No unrelated UI or functionality changes are made.
