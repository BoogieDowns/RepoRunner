The `RUNNING` plastic status light has a red pulse. That looks good.

When a status light says:

```text
STARTING
```

or:

```text
STOPPING
```

I want the peach-colored plastic light to have a **mild peach pulse**.

## Visual direction

The pulse should be:

* peach-colored
* mild
* soft
* smooth
* subtle
* slower / calm
* less intense than the red running pulse

It should feel like the light is gently active while the service is transitioning.

## Apply only to

* `STARTING`
* `STOPPING`

## Do not apply to

* `RUNNING` — keep its existing red pulse
* `STOPPED`
* `FAILED`
* `UNKNOWN`

Use the existing peach transition color family already used for Starting/Stopping.

The pulse can gently affect:

* outer glow
* border glow
* internal lens glow
* background brightness
* small light intensity

But keep it restrained.

## Important restraint

Do **not** make the peach pulse:

* flashy
* neon
* too bright
* distracting
* faster than the running pulse
* stronger than the running pulse

It should be a mild transitional indicator.
