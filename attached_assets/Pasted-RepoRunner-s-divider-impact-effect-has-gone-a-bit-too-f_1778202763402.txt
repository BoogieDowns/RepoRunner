The impact effect now dominates the screen too much.

In particular:

* the red flash is too strong
* the ripple is too strong
* the overall impact effect is drawing too much attention
* it needs to be wound back so it supports the UI instead of dominating it

Keep the current idea:

* meteor falls
* meteor hits the divider line
* divider reacts
* meteor continues downward

But make the divider reaction **smaller, quieter, and more restrained**.

---

Please reduce the red contact flash at the hit point.

That means:

* lower brightness
* lower glow intensity
* slightly smaller visual footprint
* less aggressive bloom

Keep it visible, but it should feel like a subtle energy contact rather than a bright burst.

---

## 2. Reduce the ripple strength

Please reduce the ripple effect in the divider line.

That means:

* reduce vertical amplitude
* reduce overall visibility a bit
* reduce glow strength
* keep the wave readable, but less dominant

The line should clearly react, but the reaction should feel elegant and brief.

---

## 3. Reduce the horizontal spread slightly

If the ripple is still spreading too wide, tighten it a little.

Not extremely tiny — just enough that it feels like a local disturbance rather than a major event.

---

## 4. Soften the overall intensity

Please tone down the full divider-impact package by roughly:

```text
20–35%
```

Use judgment across:

* flash intensity
* glow
* ripple amplitude
* ripple opacity
* visual dominance

The goal is not to remove the effect — only to make it feel more balanced.

---

## 5. Preserve the core behavior

Keep:

* the meteor impact happening at the divider
* the divider visibly reacting
* the meteor continuing through afterward

Just make it calmer and more integrated.
