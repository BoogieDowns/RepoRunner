## Requested change

The vertical red lines in the background currently feel like simple fading lines.

I want them to behave more like the reference image — more like **descending signal meteors**.

## Desired effect

Each falling red line should feel like:

* a **defined bright head / leading point**
* a **slightly larger or brighter tip at the front**
* a **thin trailing tail behind it**
* a **clear sense of downward motion**
* less like a line fading in/out
* more like a streak cutting downward through the dark background

Think:

* meteor streak
* signal beam streak
* falling tracer
* descending sci-fi telemetry streak

## Visual behavior guidance

Please refine the effect so that each line has:

### 1. A visible leading head

The front of the streak should have a brighter red “head” or brighter concentrated point.

This should be the clearest visual part of the falling streak.

### 2. A trailing tail

Behind the head, there should be a tail that tapers and fades out.

The tail should:

* be thinner than the head
* feel like motion blur or trailing light
* look like it is following the head downward

### 3. More directional motion

The animation should communicate that the streak is moving downward through space, not just appearing and disappearing.

The streak should feel like it is traveling, not pulsing.

### 4. Keep it subtle

Important:

* do not make the effect too bright
* do not make it look like a game effect
* do not flood the screen with too many lines
* do not make it distracting

It should remain atmospheric and elegant.

A good direction is:
