## Core problem

The current divider hit effect still feels too heavy and visually overbuilt.

It seems like there are too many layered visual elements trying to sell the impact:

* too much flash
* too much glow
* too much wave presence
* too much overall visual dominance

I want to **strip it back** and make it feel much more restrained.

## What I want instead

When a meteor hits the divider line:

1. there should be a **small red impact flash** at the hit point
2. the divider line should show a **small localized ripple**
3. the ripple should be clearly visible, but not large
4. the meteor should then continue downward
5. once below the divider, the meteor should look **clearly pixelated**

So the effect should read like:

**impact flash → small line ripple → meteor continues → meteor becomes pixelated below the line**

---

## Important: simplify the structure

Please simplify the implementation.

If there are currently multiple heavy layered ripple/flare elements, reduce it to a more restrained setup.

I do **not** want:

I want:

* one small impact flash
