Please do one **very small refinement pass** on the **Frontend / Backend status lights** only.

Do **not** redesign the UI.
Do **not** change layout.
Do **not** change functionality.
Do **not** change buttons, logs, modal, meteors, branding, service logic, IPC, or product flow.

This is only about making the current **status lights look more realistic**.

## Goal

Keep the current idea:

* rectangular / square-ish indicator light
* status text inside the light
* red illuminated treatment for `Running`
* darker unlit version for `Stopped`

But make the light feel **more like a real physical plastic indicator module**.

Right now it still reads a bit too flat / too digital.

## What to improve

### 1. Make the light feel like real translucent plastic

The status light should look like:

* a small old-school plastic indicator module
* translucent red lens material
* slightly smoked acrylic / polycarbonate feel
* realistic face depth
* subtle inner shadow and internal glow
* not just a flat red rectangle

Add a more convincing sense of:

* plastic thickness
* lens face
* shallow depth
* subtle edge bevel
* front surface reflection

## 2. Add lens detail / ribbing

The face of the light should have visible **fine horizontal lens lines / grooves / ribs**.

Think of the texture you see on real:

* old indicator lights
* warning lamps
* industrial control panel lights
* small appliance LEDs behind plastic covers

These lines should be:

* subtle but visible
* realistic
* built into the lens surface
* not decorative stripes
* not too sharp or high-contrast

## 3. Improve the highlight/reflection

Add a more believable specular highlight:

* faint glossy highlight near the upper edge or upper-left
* slight reflected sheen across the surface
* subtle not flashy
* should help it feel like real plastic catching light

Do **not** make it look wet, glassy, or overly glossy.
It should stay plastic, not chrome.

## 4. Improve the housing / frame

The light should feel like a small physical module, not just a glowing shape.

Please give it a slightly more believable structure via:

* a darker outer edge or bezel
* slight inset / framed look
* gentle shadow around the light body
* subtle separation from the card background

The frame should remain restrained and premium.

## 5. Keep the text readable and integrated

Keep the status text **inside** the light:

* `RUNNING`
* `STARTING`
* `STOPPING`
* `STOPPED`

The text should feel printed/overlaid on the illuminated module.

Please keep it:

* crisp
* readable
* centered
* visually integrated with the lens

Do not let the text get lost in the glow or texture.

## 6. Running state should feel lit, not neon

For `Running`:

* keep the red illumination
* keep the gentle pulse
* keep the glow

But shift the feeling toward:

* physical illuminated plastic
* warm internal light source
* industrial warning lamp

Not:

* sci-fi neon bar
* LED sign
* laser strip

## 7. Stopped state should feel like the same module, just unlit

For `Stopped`:

* same structure
* same lens texture
* same rectangular form

But:

* much darker
* very low or no glow
* smoked red / near-black lens look
* still readable

So it feels like the same real object in an off state.

## Important restraint

Do not make it:

* bigger
* louder
* brighter overall
* overly glossy
* toy-like
* arcade-like
* cyberpunk neon
* flat UI again

The target is:

* realistic
* tactile
* industrial
* premium
* subtle
* believable

## Preserve everything else

Do not change:

* status wording
* layout
* button shapes
* service rows
* logs
* modal
* app flow
* other UI styling

## Acceptance criteria

After this change:

1. The status lights feel more like real plastic indicator modules.
2. The lens surface has believable ribbing / fine horizontal lines.
3. The lights have more depth and material realism.
4. Running feels internally illuminated, not flat or neon.
5. Stopped feels like the same module in an unlit state.
6. The text remains inside the light and readable.
7. No unrelated UI or functionality changes are made.
